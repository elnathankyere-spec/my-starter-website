Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Do I need to provide my own text and pictures?

Yes, you provide your business content, logo, and photos. If you don't have images ready, we can guide you on finding great free alternatives or standard placeholders.

Are design revisions included if I want changes?

Yes! Up to 2 rounds of design changes and adjustments are included during the build phase to ensure your website looks exactly how you want it.

What is the difference between online hosting and a custom domain?

Hosting simply puts your website online so it can be accessed over the internet. A custom domain gives you a professional name ending like .com or .org (e.g., yourname.com).

How long does it take to complete the website?

Delivery takes 5 to 7 days once all your project content and payment arrangements have been confirmed.
